No Costs. It is agreed that the Medical Provider shall not pay any monthly fees, shall not pay any cost for the system, and shall not pay any commission for patients arriving through Safe Doc.

Safe Doc, its owners, officers, employees, affiliates and insurance agency partners shall not be liable to the Provider, to any traveler or to any third party for any claim, loss, damage or expense arising from the services. The Provider shall indemnify and hold harmless Safe Doc and the aforementioned parties against any claim, demand or proceeding brought by a traveler or any third party, and any resulting damages and reasonable legal costs, to the extent arising from the Provider's services, acts or omissions. The Provider declares and undertakes that throughout the term of this Agreement they hold valid medical licenses and professional liability (malpractice) insurance as required by local law, and shall provide proof thereof upon request.

6Independent Provider Status

The Provider acts as an independent professional and not as an employee, agent, partner or representative of Safe Doc. The Provider has no authority to bind Safe Doc or to make any statement or commitment on its behalf, and Safe Doc does not direct, control or supervise the Provider's medical work. The Provider is solely responsible for their own taxes, licenses, insurance and compliance with local law.

7Engagement of Other Practitioners

If the Provider refers a traveler to, delegates treatment to, or otherwise engages any other physician or practitioner acting on the Provider's behalf (including any associate, locum, employee or substitute), the Provider shall bear full and sole responsibility for such practitioner, including for the fact that such practitioner holds a valid medical license and professional liability insurance as required by local law, and for the quality, safety and outcome of all treatment provided by them. Any such practitioner shall be deemed to act as the Provider's agent, and the Provider shall be liable for their acts and omissions as if they were the Provider's own. The Provider shall provide proof of such license and insurance upon request. Safe Doc has no responsibility for any such practitioner, and the liability and indemnity provisions of Section 5 apply in full to their services.

8Complaints & Suspension

The Provider shall cooperate promptly and in good faith with any complaint, inquiry or claim relating to a traveler's treatment, including by providing relevant information and records to the extent permitted by law. Safe Doc may suspend the Provider's access to the platform immediately and without prior notice upon receiving a complaint, any doubt as to the Provider's license or insurance, or any suspected breach of this Agreement, pending clarification, without liability to the Provider.

9Confidentiality & Non-Circumvention

The Provider shall not circumvent the platform to provide private services to travelers referred by Safe Doc. The Provider is solely responsible for keeping travelers' medical records and personal data confidential and secure, and for complying with all applicable medical-confidentiality and data-protection laws. Safe Doc does not collect or store the medical records of treatment.
