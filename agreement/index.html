1Scope of Services

This Agreement governs the professional collaboration between Safe Doc (the "Company") and the Provider for medical consultation and treatment services provided to travelers. The Provider agrees to provide services including clinic visits, house calls, and diagnostics based on independent medical judgment.

3Service Fees & System Costs

No Costs. It is agreed that the Medical Provider shall not pay any monthly fees, shall not pay any cost for the system, and shall not pay any commission for patients arriving through Safe Doc.

4Platform Use & Payments

The Medical Provider acknowledges and agrees that any treatment of a traveler arriving through Safe Doc shall be executed through the Safe Doc application. The total payment and tests performed for the patient, including repeat treatments related to the same medical event, shall be paid through the Safe Doc platform in accordance with the agreed fair costs, without any brokerage fees whatsoever.

5Medical Responsibility & Liability

The Provider bears sole medical and legal responsibility for the treatment. Safe Doc is a technology platform and shall not be held liable for any medical outcomes, malpractice, or negligence. The Provider declares they possess valid medical licenses and professional indemnity insurance required by local law.

6Confidentiality & Non-Circumvention

The Provider shall maintain absolute confidentiality regarding the Company's business model and patient data. The Provider shall not circumvent the platform to provide private services to travelers referred by Safe Doc.

7Term & Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing 30 days' written notice. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the country where the medical service is provided. Disputes shall be resolved through good-faith negotiations.
